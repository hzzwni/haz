Hi! My name is An Nur Hazwani, and people call me Wani. I'm from Setapak, Kuala Lumpur. I’m currently studying Digital Media Communication Technology at UiTM Alor Gajah, Melaka, and I’ve always had a huge passion for content creation and digital marketing. What I love most is mixing creative ideas with technical skills to make visual content that actually catches people’s attention.

I’m also deeply into photography, and snapping random photos of things around me, especially the sky.
